# 404 page: scene script

The animated scene on the 404 page ("404 site not reached"). Generated ASCII art, animated with CSS only, plays once and never loops.

## Cast and set

- **Kitty:** a stick figure with a Hello Kitty head. The head floats just above the body, like the walking-man reference. Ears are wide and close together, and the bow is on the ear that is on the viewer's right when she faces the viewer.
- **Bus stop sign:** on the left, a framed board showing a bus and "404", with a timetable plate, a pole and a base. It never moves.
- **Ground:** a thin line along the bottom. The sidebar is on the left edge of the stage; Kitty walks out from behind it.

## Rules

- Kitty is visible for the whole scene. She never disappears, including after the animation ends.
- The cigarette is always visible once it exists. The hand holds it between two fingers and never covers it. It only gets shorter while it is visibly burning at her mouth.
- Cigarette: thin, pointing slightly downward (about 24 degrees), never straight out.
- She faces right while walking and lighting up, and faces the viewer from the turn onwards.
- The animation plays once and ends in the final pose. It does not refresh or restart.
- With "reduce motion" turned on, the final pose is shown straight away.

## Timeline

| # | Time | Beat | What it looks like |
|---|------|------|--------------------|
| 1 | 0 to 7 s | Walk in | Kitty walks left to right from behind the sidebar, seen from the side, head turned right. Bent knees, swinging arms, body bobbing. She passes in front of the sign. |
| 2 | 7 to 8 s | Stop | She stops just to the right of the sign, still facing right, arms down. |
| 3 | 8 to 9 s | Get the cigarette | Her right hand goes to her side, takes a cigarette from a small pack in her pocket, and puts it in her mouth. |
| 4 | 9 to 11 s | Light it | Her other hand raises a lighter and the flame flickers. The tip of the cigarette turns red. She lowers the lighter and a first puff of smoke rises. |
| 5 | 11 to 12 s | Turn to the viewer | She turns in three steps (side view, three-quarter view, front view) until she faces the viewer. |
| 6 | 12 to about 21 s | Smoke it | Three or four drags, see below. |
| 7 | about 21 to 22 s | Throw the butt | With a short butt left, her arm swings out to the side and flicks it. It arcs through the air still glowing and lands on the ground to her right. |
| 8 | about 22 to 24 s | Stomp it out | She lifts her foot over the butt and stamps down three times with a small twist. A last wisp of smoke rises, the red glow turns grey, and she puts her foot back. |
| 9 | 24 s onwards | Hold | She faces the viewer with arms at her sides, completely still. The extinguished butt stays on the ground. The sign stays on the left. Nothing loops. |

### Beat 6 in detail: one drag

1. Her hand brings the cigarette to her mouth, pinched between two fingers. The whole cigarette and the glowing tip stay visible, with the hand below or beside it.
2. While it is at her mouth the tip glows brighter and the cigarette burns shorter in two visible steps.
3. She lowers it, holding it by the filter, and a puff of smoke rises from her mouth.
4. Once during the scene she taps it with a finger and a few grey flecks of ash fall.

## Open decisions

- **Where the cigarette comes from:** a small pack in her pocket (default), or already behind her ear.
- **Final pose:** completely still (default), or a tiny idle such as blinking or a tail twitch.

## Known bugs in the current version

- Kitty disappears when the timeline ends. Probable cause: the last pose has no defined end state, so the browser falls back to hidden when the animation finishes. To be confirmed when fixing.
- During drags the hand is drawn over the cigarette, then the next frame shows a shorter one with nothing visible in between.

## Size note

The page is about 520 KB with 46 frames. This script adds roughly 15 frames, so expect about 650 KB. It is only loaded on a missing page.

## Files

- `layouts/404.html`: the scene markup and frame keyframes.
- `assets/css/main.css`: the `/* 404 */` block.
- The frames are generated by scripts kept in the session scratchpad, not in the repo.
