This policy describes how Tietoteekkariyhdistys ry (business ID 3381706-8, Konemiehentie 2, 02150 Espoo) handles personal data. The controller is the association, represented by its board. Contact: hallitus@tietoteekkariyhdistys.fi.

## Member register

The association's ordinary members are its member guilds, which are organisations. The personal data of a guild's own members is kept by that guild, not by Tietoteekkariyhdistys, and we do not receive it. At the moment the association has no individual or support members, so it does not hold a register of persons.

If you apply to become an individual or support member by email, the Finnish Associations Act (yhdistyslaki) requires the association to keep a member register. In that case the board collects:

- your full name (legally required)
- your official place of residence (legally required)
- your email address, which is visible to the board when you write to it
- for individual members, which member guild you belong to

The data is used only to process and accept membership applications, to keep the member register, to communicate with members and to invoice membership fees. The legal basis is the association's legal obligation to keep a member register and your own request to join. The data is not disclosed to third parties, except where the law requires it, for example to authorities. It is kept while you are a member and afterwards only as long as the law or the association's accounting requires.

## This website

This website does not use cookies, analytics or advertising, and it does not load scripts, fonts or other content from third parties. We do not collect personal data through the website itself. The names and association email addresses of the board members are published on the website so that they can be contacted in their role. The site is served by a web hosting provider, which may process technical data such as IP addresses when pages are requested.

## Your rights

You have the right to ask what data we hold about you, to have incorrect data corrected, and, where the law allows, to have data erased or its processing restricted. You can also complain to the Office of the Data Protection Ombudsman (tietosuoja.fi). To exercise your rights, email hallitus@tietoteekkariyhdistys.fi.
