Tietoteekkariyhdistys (TTY) is an umbrella organization for all of Finland's IT student guilds, founded in 2023 during the Titeenien Taistot event in Turku. You can read our purpose on the front page.

## History

Planning for TTY began in 2021 at a Muistinnollaus sillis, where the TTY group was formed. The founding documents were written at the sillis table. The process then took about a year, during which the guilds were persuaded to join.

- **10 April 2022**: the TTY group met in Aitoo, at TiTe's sillis. Representatives from TiK, Cluster, TiTe, OTiT and Digit agreed to take the bylaws to their guild boards for approval, and everyone present was added to the TTY group.
- **4 November 2022**: Tietokilta's meeting approved the proposal for its board to make the guild a member of TTY.
- **2023**: the founding meeting was held at Titeenien Taistot in Turku, and the founding charter was signed.
